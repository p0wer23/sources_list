# Sources List Intent

Local Android app for saving URLs into:
- `Unclassified`
- `Casual`
- `Serious`

V1:
- home is selector-only with `+`
- add from `+`
- open, copy, move, delete
- open Substack links in the Substack app when available
- done/restore for `Casual` and `Serious`
- ranked `P1`/`P2`/`P3` priorities for active `Casual` and `Serious`
- `Serious` opens group cards instead of a flat link list
- `Serious` has a built-in `Ungrouped` group plus custom groups
- `Serious` group cards support their own `P1`/`P2`/`P3` ranking
- active links inside each `Serious` group also support `P1`/`P2`/`P3`
- completed URLs cannot move
